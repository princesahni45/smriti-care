## 1. Executive Status & Honesty Assessment

- **Was the model loaded?**
  - **Cold baseline status:** The physical model weight file (qwen3-0.6b-q4_k_m.bin / qwen3-0.6b-q4_k_m.gguf) is **not installed by default inside the APK**.
  - **Honesty Assessment:** The application reports AIModelStatus.notInstalled during initial startup and does not claim that Qwen is executing when binary weights are missing.
  - When model binary weights are absent, all conversational assistance gracefully degrades to the deterministic dementia-safe AIFallbackService.
  - When model weights are present in internal storage (/data/user/0/com.smriticare.smriti_care/files/qwen3-0.6b-q4_k_m.bin), the native Android bridge successfully loads the model into resident RAM, transitioning the state to AIModelStatus.ready.

## 3. Supported Proof of Concept Capabilities

For this stage, the local Qwen on-device AI integration is strictly limited to 5 non-critical conversation types:

1. **Warm Greeting**:
   - Comforting, predictable hello addressing the senior by name.
   - Example: *Hello Ramesh, it is wonderful to see you. You are in a safe, peaceful place.*
2. **Game Explanation**:
   - Calming instructions for cognitive memory match and word recall activities.
   - Example: *Memory Match is a gentle activity to exercise your memory. Take all the time you need; there is no hurry.*
3. **Repeating a Reminder**:
   - Neutral confirmation of scheduled medicine or water intake without clinical modifications.
   - Example: *Here is your reminder: Blood Pressure Medicine scheduled for 8:30 AM. Everything is organized for you.*
4. **Answering Simple Non-Medical Questions**:
   - Conversational reassurance about the day, weather, or familiar surroundings.
5. **Caregiver Assistance Guidance**:
   - Guiding the patient to connect directly with their caregiver whenever they feel confused or anxious.
   - Example: *If you need help with anything, please let your caregiver know. They are here to support you.*

## 4. Safety Guardrails & Validation Policy

Every response generated by on-device Qwen passes through the AIResponseValidator before being displayed:

- **No Medical Diagnoses**: Phrases mentioning disease stages, terminal diagnoses, or dementia progression are rejected.
- **No Medication Dosage Advice**: Any mention of milligrams (mg), milliliters (ml), or advice to increase, skip, or alter medications is stripped and replaced with caregiver referral guidance.
- **No Emergency Decisions**: Qwen is strictly prohibited from evaluating or canceling emergency calls or advising against medical help.
- **No False History or Confabulation**: Responses are limited to 1-2 calming sentences (maximum 350 characters).
- **Safety Fallback**: If an invalid response is caught, AIResponseValidator substitutes a calm, standardized fallback directing the patient to their caregiver.

---

## 5. Explicit System Limitations

1. **No Emergency SOS Control**: Emergency SOS triggering and dispatch remain 100% hardcoded and deterministic.
2. **No Medicine Scheduling Engine**: Medicine schedules and dosage logs are managed exclusively by the caregiver via CaregiverService. Qwen cannot modify reminders.
3. **No Cognitive Clinical Scoring**: Cognitive scoring in games is calculated strictly via deterministic algorithms (ccuracy, completionTimeSeconds).
4. **Offline Assamese Recognition Disclaimer**: Assamese text-to-speech and text generation are supported through fallback and prompt engineering, but native offline Assamese automatic speech recognition (ASR) is not yet active (planned via AI4Bharat).
